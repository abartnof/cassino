# Welcome to Cassino

## Your goal

- Take cards from the table. Points come from the most cards, the most spades, each ace, and the 10 of diamonds and 2 of spades.
- The first to **21** points wins.

## What's in front of you

- **Your cards** at the bottom; your opponent's, face down, across the table.
- **The table:** the cards face up in the middle.
- **The score** at the top, and **?** to see these pages again.

## On your turn

Play one card from your hand, and:

- **Take** table cards that match it or add up to it,
- **Build** a total with table cards, to take on a later turn, or
- **Trail:** lay it on the table, taking nothing.

## How

- When it's your turn, the **Build**, **Take** and **Trail** buttons appear.
- Tap a card in your hand, then any table cards to use with it.
- Cards you can use are outlined; the rest dim.
- Then press the button for your move.

## Your opponent

- Takes turns with you, one card each: watch what they take and build.
- What they say appears beside their cards.

## How a game goes

- Four cards each, then four more, until the pack is used: six deals make a **hand**.
- Points are counted at the end of each hand.

# Taking a match

## What you can do

- A card takes the same card from the table: a **7 takes a 7**.

## How

- Tap your 7, tap the 7 on the table, then **Take**.

## Good to know

- It can take every match at once: a 7 takes two 7s.
- In Classic, a jack, queen or king takes one of its kind at a time.
- You never have to take, and you can leave cards behind.

# Taking cards that add up

## What you can do

- A card takes table cards that **add up to it**: a 9 takes a 4 and a 5.

## How

- Tap your 9, tap the 4 and the 5, then **Take**.

## Good to know

- It can take several sets at once: 4 + 5, **and** 6 + 3, **and** a 9.
- An ace counts 1. In Classic, jacks, queens and kings never add up.

# Building

## What it is

- A pile you make now and take with one card on a later turn.
- The badge on a build shows its total.

## How

- Holding a 3 and an 8, with a 5 on the table: tap the 3, tap the 5, then **Build 8**.
- Next turn, your 8 takes the pile.

## The rules

- You must hold a card of the total. No 8, no build of 8.
- Only a card of the total takes the build, and it takes the whole pile.
- While you have a build, you can't trail: take something, or build again.

## Your opponent

- They can take it with a card of its total, and now know you hold one.
- A build belongs to whoever built on it last.

# Raising a build

## What it is

- Adding a card from your hand to a build, for a bigger total.

## How

- Holding an ace and a 7, with their build of 6: tap the ace, tap the build, then **Build 7**.
- It's yours now, and your 7 takes it.

## The rules

- You must hold a card of the new total.
- Only the card from your hand raises it, not table cards.
- A multiple build can't be raised.

## Rather not?

- Turn off **Raise builds** in the new game's menu (the + at the top).

# Multiple builds

## What it is

- A build holding more than one set of its total.

## How

- Holding two 8s, with a 5 and a 3 on the table: tap an 8, the 5 and the 3, then **Build 8s**.
- Your other 8 takes it all.

## Good to know

- You can add a set to any build, yours or your opponent's.
- It can't be raised: its total stays until it is taken.
- Its badge reads **8s**, and you'll hear "Building eights."

# Royal Cassino

## What's different

- Jacks, queens and kings have numbers: **jack 11, queen 12, king 13**.
- They match, add up and build like any card: a king takes a queen and an ace.
- Builds go up to 13. Scoring is unchanged.

## Aces: 1 or 14

- With **Aces count 1 or 14** on in the new game's menu, an ace from your hand takes as 1 or 14.
- As 14, it takes a 9 and a 5, or a king and an ace.
- When both work, the buttons read **Take as 1** and **Take as 14**.
- On the table and in builds, an ace counts 1.
- Holding an ace, you can build 14.

# Scoring a hand

## Points, each hand

- **Most cards:** 3. 27 is sure to be most; at 26 each, nobody scores.
- **Most spades:** 1. 7 is sure to be most.
- **Big Cassino** (the 10 of diamonds): 2.
- **Little Cassino** (the 2 of spades): 1.
- **Each ace:** 1.
- **Each sweep** (taking every card on the table): 1, if this game scores sweeps.

## Good to know

- Cards left on the table at the end go to whoever took cards last.
- The first to **21** wins.
- Both reach 21 in one hand: the higher total wins; a tie plays another hand.
- The score's arrow opens each hand's points.

# Table talk

## The calls

- **"Low deals."** The lower cut card deals first.
- **"Building eight."** A build of 8. **"Building eights."** A multiple build.
- A raise is called by its new total: **"Nine."**
- **"Last."** The last deal of the hand.
- **"Clear!"** A sweep: every card on the table taken.
- **"Cash."** An ace taking an ace.
- **"That's the cards."**, **"Seven spades."** A point already sure: 27 cards, or 7 spades.
- **"You left the five."** A card you could have taken.

## How much is said

- **Table talk** in the settings: **Everything** (the players chat), **The calls**, or **Quiet**.
